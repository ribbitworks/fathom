# Contributing to Fathom

Thank you for your interest in Fathom. It is in early development, and it isn't accepting outside contributions yet.

## Pull Requests

Pull requests from outside the project are closed without review. This isn't a judgement of the work: while Fathom's design is still settling, the maintainer keeps every change on one path, from design to review, as the [Agent Workflow](docs/standards/shared/agent-workflow.md) describes.

## Issues and Questions

Issues and questions come from people the maintainer invites into the `ribbitworks` organization on GitHub, who can file and sort issues there. There is no way to ask to be invited yet. Issues from people who weren't invited may be closed without a reply.

Invited people file issues through the forms on the [New issue](https://github.com/jlarue26/fathom/issues/new/choose) page, as [Issues](docs/issues.md) describes.

## Security

Never report a vulnerability in an issue. Follow the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Conduct

Everyone taking part in Fathom follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
