# Access history

Access history lists who signed in, who changed accounts, roles and settings, and who viewed, changed or exported private details, newest first. Roles that see administration see the page, under **History**.

## Main Tasks

### Find an Entry

Type in **Search access history**, such as a name, an email address, or a word such as `switched`.

### Check What Deserves a Second Look

Look for the yellow dots. They mark changes that could give access or take it away: a new account, a change to an account's roles or to a role, a password reset, a change to the rules for passwords, a deactivation, someone changing their own name, and a move of data to another class.

## Marks and Terms

- **An entry** gives the date and time, then a sentence: who, what they did, and to what, such as an Administrator who **reactivated the account for** someone. An email address follows each account's name, to tell accounts apart.
- **A yellow dot** before an entry means it deserves a second look.
- **Viewing private details**, Personal or Confidential, is recorded at most once a day for each account, without naming whose details, and so is viewing the hiring plan. The day follows Fathom's time zone, set in [Settings](settings.md).
- **An export** names the table, and the Personal and Confidential data the file held.
- **Changes to private details** are recorded without their values, and name the person only to roles that see people. Changes to the time zone and the rules for passwords show both the old and the new.
- **Fathom's operators** made a change from outside Fathom, such as installing it.
- **A removed account** made a change from an account since deleted.
- The page holds the latest 200 entries.

## Where Changes Go

Access history only reads, and changes nothing. Changes to the plan, people and configuration are in [Change history](change-history.md).
