# Access history

**Access history** lists, newest first, who signed in, who changed accounts, roles and settings, and who viewed, changed or exported private details. You find it under **History** in the left-hand menu, if your role sees administration.

## What to Know First

- **Private details** are data in the **Personal** or **Confidential** class, which only some roles see. See [Data classes](data-classes.md).
- **Times** show in your own browser's time zone. Fathom's own time zone, which an Administrator sets on **Settings**, decides only what counts as one day for the once-a-day entries below.

## Main Tasks

### Find an Entry

Type in **Search access history**: a name, an email address, or a word such as `switched`. The search looks only through the latest 200 entries, which are all the page holds.

You're done when you find the entry you want.

### Check What Deserves a Second Look

Look for the entries ending in a grey **Worth checking** box. They mark changes that could give someone access or take it away:

- a new account;
- a change to an account's roles, or to a role;
- a password reset;
- a change to the rules for passwords;
- a deactivation;
- someone changing their own name;
- a move of data to another class.

You're done when you've checked each entry marked **Worth checking**.

## Marks and Terms

- **An entry** gives the date and time, then a sentence: who did what, and to what. For example, an Administrator **reactivated the account for** someone. An email address follows each account's name, so you can tell accounts apart.
- **Worth checking**, in a grey box after an entry, means it deserves a second look.
- **Viewing private details** is recorded at most once a day for each account. The entry doesn't name whose details they were. Viewing the hiring plan, your organization's planned hires, is recorded the same way.
- **An export** is recorded only when the file held Personal or Confidential data. The entry names the table, and that data.
- **A change to private details** is recorded without its values. It names the person only to roles that see people.
- **A change to the time zone or the rules for passwords** shows both the old and the new.
- **Fathom's operators** made a change from outside Fathom, such as installing it.
- **A removed account** made a change from an account that's since been deleted.

## Where Changes Go

You can't change anything on this page. It only shows what happened. Changes to the plan, people and configuration are in [Change history](change-history.md).
