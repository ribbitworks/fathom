# Change history

**Change history**, under **History** in the left-hand menu, lists every change saved to the plan, people and configuration, newest first. Each entry says who made the change and what changed, with the old and new values. You see only the changes to data you can see.

## Main Tasks

### Find a Change

1. Type in the **Search** box at the top of the page: a person's name, a value, or who made the change.
1. To see older entries, select **Show more** at the end of the page. It works while you're searching too.

You're done when you find the entry you want.

### Read an Entry

An entry has a sentence for each record the save changed, such as **changed the list Sites: Name from Sites to Locations**. Records of one kind added or deleted together share a sentence, such as **added 3 choices to Sites**.

When a save changed many records, a button such as **Show 3 more** lists the rest. **Show all** lists every name in a line.

## Marks and Terms

- **Undoing a change** adds a new entry that puts the old values back. Nothing is ever taken out of the history.
- **A rejection** is left out when the draft it rejected held details you can't see, or named someone you don't see, since its reason may repeat them.

## Where Changes Go

You can't change anything on this page. It only shows what changed. See [Drafts](drafts.md) for changes waiting for approval.
