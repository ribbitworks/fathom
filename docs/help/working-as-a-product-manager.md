# Working as a Product Manager

A Product Manager shapes the plan: the projects customers need, the proposals that may become projects, the features they need, and the releases that ship them. Product Managers also approve others' drafts of the plan and releases.

## Plan a Feature

1. On **Features**, add the feature, as [Features](features.md#add-a-feature) sets out.
1. Choose its **Target cycle**, as [Features](features.md#plan-when-a-feature-ships) sets out.
1. Give it to a team in **Feature team**, and a **Needed by** month if it's wanted by then.
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Tie Features to Projects and Proposals

1. On **Projects**, choose the features a project or proposal needs, as [Projects](projects.md#choose-the-features-a-project-needs) sets out.
1. Say why a feature was removed from a proposal, as [Projects](projects.md#give-a-reason-for-removing-a-feature-from-a-proposal) sets out.
1. Open a project's or proposal's details to read its **Scope history**, as [Projects](projects.md#see-how-a-projects-or-proposals-scope-changed) sets out.

## Keep Projects and Customers Current

1. On **Projects**, add the customers, proposals and projects, as [Projects](projects.md#add-a-project-proposal-customer-or-milestone) sets out.
1. Change a proposal's **Status** as it's won, lost or withdrawn.
1. Add each project's milestones, marking those the customer receives, as [Projects](projects.md#add-a-milestone-to-a-project) sets out.

## Make a Release

1. On **Releases**, select the stream, and make the release from its cycles, as [Releases](releases.md#make-a-release) sets out.
1. Write what it ships in its note, as [Releases](releases.md#write-a-releases-note) sets out.
1. Group **Features** by **Release** to see what it holds, as [Features](features.md#group-the-table) sets out.

## Approve Others' Drafts

Approve or reject drafts of projects, proposals, features and releases on the **Drafts** page, as [How Work Moves Between Roles](how-work-moves-between-roles.md#approve-the-drafts) sets out. A Product Manager's own drafts need another approver.
