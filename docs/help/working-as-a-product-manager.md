# Working as a Product Manager

As a Product Manager, you shape the plan: the features to build, the projects customers need, and the proposals that may become projects. You also add columns to Features, and approve other people's drafts of the plan, features and releases.

Your pages are **Features** and **Projects**, under **Plan** in the left-hand menu, and **Drafts**, under **History**.

## What to Know First

- **Features:** a feature is a body of work that one feature team delivers. It targets a cycle, a planning period such as a quarter.
- **Projects and proposals:** a project is work delivered to customers. A proposal is work offered or requested that may become a project. A project's milestones are its dated points, such as a delivery to the customer.
- **Drafts:** your changes are drafts. Nobody else sees them until you select **Publish** in the top bar, and they go live once someone approves them. Your own drafts need another approver, such as a Project Manager or the Engineering Director.

## Plan the Features

Once you've made your changes, select **Publish** in the top bar.

### Add a Feature

1. On **Features**, select **New feature**.
1. Type the feature's **Name**.
1. Select **Add**.

To add one quickly, type its name in the empty row at the end of the table, and press Enter. See [Features](features.md#add-a-feature).

### Say When a Feature Is Needed

Type the month it's wanted in its **Needed by**, as digits, such as `2027-03`. It's a rough wish; **Target cycle** is the commitment. See [Features](features.md#marks-and-terms).

### Choose the Target Cycle and Team

1. Choose the cycle the feature is planned to ship in, in its **Target cycle**. **Release** then names the release that covers it.

A Feature Lead or an Engineering Manager chooses the team that builds it, in its **Feature team**, since your role doesn't change a feature's team.

See [Features](features.md#plan-when-a-feature-ships).

### Add a Column to Features

1. On **Features**, select **New column**, at the right of the page's heading.
1. Type the column's **Name**.
1. Choose its **Type**.
1. Select **Add column**.

You can fill its cells straight away. See [Features](features.md#add-a-column).

## Keep Projects and Proposals Current

Once you've made your changes, select **Publish** in the top bar.

### Add Customers, Proposals and Projects

1. On **Projects**, select the **Customers**, **Proposals** or **Projects** tab.
1. Select **New customer**, **New proposal** or **New project**.
1. Fill in the form, and select **Add**.

See [Projects](projects.md#add-a-project-proposal-customer-or-milestone).

### Change a Proposal's Status

As a proposal is won, lost or withdrawn, choose its new **Status** on the **Proposals** tab. See [Projects](projects.md#marks-and-terms).

### Add Milestones to a Project

1. Open the project's details.
1. Select **Add milestone**.
1. Type its **Name** and **Date**.
1. If the customer receives something then, tick **Customer delivery**.
1. Select **Add milestone**.

See [Projects](projects.md#add-a-milestone-to-a-project).

## Tie Features to Projects and Proposals

### Choose the Features a Project Needs

1. On **Projects**, select the project's **Features** cell, and press Enter.
1. Pick each feature the project needs.
1. Press Enter again to finish.
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

You choose a proposal's features the same way. See [Projects](projects.md#choose-the-features-a-project-needs).

### Say Why a Feature Leaves a Proposal

1. Open the proposal's details.
1. Under **Scope history**, select the removal's row.
1. Type the reason.
1. Press Enter.

You can give a reason only while the removal is still a draft. See [Projects](projects.md#give-a-reason-for-removing-a-feature-from-a-proposal).

### Read the Scope History

Open a project's or proposal's details. **Scope history**, at the end, lists each feature added or removed, and when, newest first. See [Projects](projects.md#see-how-a-projects-or-proposals-scope-changed).

## Approve or Reject Drafts

You approve or reject drafts of projects, proposals, features, releases and schedules. Drafts you changed yourself stay waiting for another approver.

### Approve on the Drafts Page

1. Select the **Drafts** page, under **History**, then **To decide**.
1. Tick each draft you want to approve.
1. Select **Approve** at the right of the page's heading. It names the count, such as **Approve 3 drafts…**.
1. Check the dialog, which names any other drafts approved with them.
1. Select **Approve**.

See [Drafts page](drafts-page.md#approve-drafts).

### Approve All a Table Shows

On a table such as **Features**, select **Approve** in its **Draft** menu. It names the count, such as **Approve 4 drafts…**, and approves every waiting draft the table shows. See [Drafts](drafts.md#what-the-draft-menu-shows).

### Reject a Draft With a Reason

1. On **To decide**, tick each draft you want to reject.
1. Select **Reject…** at the right of the page's heading.
1. Type the **Reason**.
1. Select **Reject**.

Each draft goes back to whoever made it, with your reason. See [Drafts page](drafts-page.md#reject-drafts).

You're done when your features, projects and proposals show what's planned, each change approved.
