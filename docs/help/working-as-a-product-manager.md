# Working as a Product Manager

As a Product Manager, you shape the plan. That's the projects customers need, the proposals that may become projects, the features they need, and the releases that ship them. You also approve other people's drafts of the plan and releases.

## What to Know First

- **Features:** a feature is a body of work that one feature team delivers. It targets a cycle, a planning period such as a quarter.
- **Releases:** a release ships the features that target its cycles. Each release belongs to a release stream, such as one product line's.
- **Projects and proposals:** a project is work delivered to customers. A proposal is work offered or requested that may become a project. A project's milestones are its dated points, such as a delivery to the customer.
- **Drafts:** your changes are drafts. Nobody else sees them until you select **Publish** in the top bar, and they go live once someone approves them. Your own drafts need another approver, such as a Project Manager or the Engineering Director.

## Plan a Feature

1. On **Features**, add the feature. See [Features](features.md#add-a-feature).
1. Choose the cycle it's planned to ship in, in its **Target cycle**. See [Features](features.md#plan-when-a-feature-ships).
1. Choose the team that builds it, in its **Feature team**.
1. If it's wanted by a certain month, type that month in **Needed by**.
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Tie Features to Projects and Proposals

1. On **Projects**, choose the features each project or proposal needs. See [Projects](projects.md#choose-the-features-a-project-needs).
1. If you take a feature off a proposal, say why. See [Projects](projects.md#give-a-reason-for-removing-a-feature-from-a-proposal).
1. To see each feature added or removed, and when, read the **Scope history** in a project's or proposal's details. See [Projects](projects.md#see-how-a-projects-or-proposals-scope-changed).

## Keep Projects and Customers Current

1. On **Projects**, add the customers, proposals and projects. See [Projects](projects.md#add-a-project-proposal-customer-or-milestone).
1. As a proposal is won, lost or withdrawn, change its **Status**.
1. Add each project's milestones. Tick **Customer delivery** on those the customer receives. See [Projects](projects.md#add-a-milestone-to-a-project).
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Make a Release

1. On **Releases**, open the stream, and make the release from its cycles. See [Releases](releases.md#make-a-release).
1. Write what the release ships in its note. See [Releases](releases.md#write-a-releases-note).
1. To see what it holds, group **Features** by **Release**. See [Features](features.md#group-the-table).
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Approve Others' Drafts

On the **Drafts** page, approve or reject drafts of projects, proposals, features and releases. See [Drafts page](drafts-page.md#approve-drafts).

You're done when your features, projects and releases show what's planned, each change approved.
