# Overview

Overview is the page Fathom opens on when you sign in, whatever your role. It greets you by name and says which role you're acting as, then lists every job your role's permissions allow, under **Plan**, **People**, **Capacity**, **Administration**, **Drafts** and **History**.

[How Fathom fits together](how-fathom-fits-together.md) shows how the plan, its releases and its people connect.

## Your Role's Jobs

The jobs are the sections of the Help pages for the roles Fathom starts with, such as [Working as a Product Manager](working-as-a-product-manager.md), so the two always match. Each shows once, with only the tasks your permissions allow, under **Plan**, **People**, **Capacity**, **Administration**, **Drafts** or **History**. A role made in Fathom, or a copy of another, sees the jobs its permissions allow too.

- **A job:** select its name to see its finer tasks, and again to hide them.
- **Where it's done:** the link beside a job or task, or under it on a narrow screen, opens the page where you do it, on the right tab, such as **Drafts › To decide**.
- **Help:** each job's and task's **Help** link opens its section of your role's Help page, or of another starting role's when yours doesn't have it.

If you hold several roles, the Overview lists the jobs of the role you're acting as. Switch roles in the account menu, at the top right, to see another's.
