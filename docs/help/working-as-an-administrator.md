# Working as an Administrator

As an Administrator, you manage who can sign in to Fathom and what each role lets them do. You also set Fathom's settings. Your role sees neither the plan nor people's details. Your pages are under **Administration** in the left-hand menu, with **Access history** under **History**.

## Make an Account

1. Select the **Accounts** page.
1. Select **New account**.
1. Fill in the person's name, email address and roles.
1. Select **Create account**.
1. Copy the password Fathom shows, and give it to its holder. They change it when they first sign in.

See [Accounts](accounts.md#make-an-account).

## Look After Accounts

On **Accounts**, select **Edit** in an account's row. From there you can change its name or roles, reset its password, or deactivate it. See [Accounts](accounts.md).

## Change What a Role Lets Its Holders Do

1. Select the **Roles** page.
1. Select **Edit** in the role's row.
1. Tick or untick boxes in the grid.
1. Select **Save**.
1. Check what each account holding the role gains and loses.
1. Select **Save changes**.

To make a new role, select **Copy** in the row of the closest role, and change the copy. See [Roles](roles.md).

## Change the Settings

Select the **Settings** page to set Fathom's time zone and the rules for passwords. See [Settings](settings.md).

## Check the Access History

Select the **Access history** page. Look for entries with a yellow dot before them: those are worth a second look, such as a change to someone's roles. See [Access history](access-history.md#check-what-deserves-a-second-look).

You're done when everyone who needs Fathom can sign in with the right roles.
