# Working as an Administrator

As an Administrator, you manage who can sign in to Fathom and what each role lets them do. You also set Fathom's settings. Your role sees neither the plan nor people's details. Your pages are under **Administration** in the left-hand menu, with **Access history** under **History**.

## What to Know First

- **Accounts and roles:** each account holds one or more roles, and a role decides what its holder sees and does.
- **No drafts:** your changes to accounts, roles and settings take effect straight away. Nobody approves them, and each one is recorded in the access history.

## Make an Account

1. Select the **Accounts** page.
1. Select **New account**.
1. Type the person's **Name**.
1. Type their **Email address**.
1. Tick each role the account holds.
1. If you ticked more than one, choose the **Default role**.
1. Select **Create account**.
1. Select **Copy** to copy the password Fathom shows.
1. Give the password to its holder. They change it when they first sign in.
1. Select **Done**.

The account is ready once its row shows **Active**. See [Accounts](accounts.md#make-an-account).

## Look After Accounts

On **Accounts**, open an account's details from the button at the right of its **Name**. Under them, **Edit…** changes its name or roles, **Reset password…** gives it a new password, and **Deactivate…** stops it signing in. A deactivated account has **Reactivate…** there instead. To find accounts by status, filter the **Status** column. See [Accounts](accounts.md).

## Change What a Role Lets Its Holders Do

1. Select the **Roles** page.
1. Select **Edit** in the role's row.
1. Tick or untick boxes in the grid.
1. Select **Save**.
1. Check what each account holding the role gains and loses.
1. Select **Save changes**.

To make a new role, select **Copy** in the row of the closest role, and change the copy. To put back a starting role's own name, description and boxes, select **Restore template** while you edit it. See [Roles](roles.md).

## Change the Settings

On the **Settings** page, set Fathom's time zone and the rules for passwords. See [Settings](settings.md).

## Check the Access History

1. Select the **Access history** page.
1. Look for entries ending in a grey **Worth checking** box. Those deserve a second look, such as a change to someone's roles. See [Access history](access-history.md#check-what-deserves-a-second-look).

You're done when everyone who needs Fathom can sign in with the right roles.
