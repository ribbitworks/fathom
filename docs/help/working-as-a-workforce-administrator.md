# Working as a Workforce Administrator

As a Workforce Administrator, you keep Fathom's records: its people and planned hires, reporting lines, feature teams, lists and planning settings. Engineering Operations Managers hold this role. If Fathom is brand new, start with [Setting Up Fathom](setting-up-fathom.md).

## What to Know First

- **Drafts:** most of your changes are drafts. Nobody else sees them until you select **Publish** in the top bar. They go live once a Project Manager or the Engineering Director approves them.
- **Planned hires:** a planned hire is a job you plan to fill. Its **State** says where the hire stands, such as **Request to hire** or **Approved to hire**.
- **Feature teams:** a feature team is a group that works on features together, whoever its members report to.

## Keep People Current

1. On **People**, add each new starter. See [People](people.md#add-a-person-or-a-planned-hire).
1. Give someone who leaves an **End** date. Don't remove them. See [People](people.md#remove-a-person-or-planned-hire).
1. When someone moves, change their **Manager**. If the new manager hasn't managed before, tick their **Manager?** and get that approved first, in a round of its own. See [People](people.md#give-someone-a-new-manager).
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Keep the Hiring Plan

A planned hire goes through three rounds, each published and approved before the next.

1. On **People**, add the planned hire with a hiring state, such as **Request to hire**. See [People](people.md#add-a-person-or-a-planned-hire).
1. Select **Publish**, and wait for the draft to be approved.
1. Once the hire is agreed, change its **State** to one that means approved, such as **Approved to hire**.
1. Select **Publish**, and wait for the draft to be approved.
1. Once someone is hired, fill the planned hire with them. See [People](people.md#fill-a-planned-hire).
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Keep the Lists Current

1. On **Lists**, add choices as they're needed. See [Lists](lists.md#add-choices).
1. Retire a choice that's no longer used. See [Lists](lists.md#retire-or-remove-a-choice).
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Keep the Feature Teams Current

1. On **Feature assignments**, move people between teams from the month they move. See [Feature assignments](feature-assignments.md#put-someone-on-a-feature-team-from-a-month).
1. To add, rename or remove a team, use the **Org chart**. See [Org chart](org-chart.md#change-a-feature-team).
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Keep the Planning Settings

1. On **Capacity**, choose **Settings**, and change the settings. See [Capacity](capacity.md#change-a-setting).
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

Once the change is approved, a change to the hours or working days changes what estimates on **Features** read in person-days, weeks and months.

## Decide Who Sees What

Each detail, column and list has a class, which decides who sees it. On **Data classes**, under **Administration**, you can change a class. It takes effect straight away, with no draft. See [Data classes](data-classes.md#change-a-class).

You're done when **People** shows everyone with their right manager and team, and nothing of yours is waiting for approval.
