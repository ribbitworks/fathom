# Working as a Workforce Administrator

A Workforce Administrator keeps the organization's records: its people and planned hires, reporting lines, feature teams, lists and planning settings. Engineering Operations Managers hold this role. Most of their changes are drafts that a Project Manager or the Engineering Director approves. A new Fathom is set up as [Setting Up Fathom](setting-up-fathom.md) sets out.

## Keep People Current

1. On **People**, add each new starter, as [People](people.md#add-a-person-or-a-planned-hire) sets out.
1. Give someone who leaves an **End** date, as [People](people.md#remove-a-person-or-planned-hire) sets out.
1. Change someone's **Manager** when they move, as [People](people.md#give-someone-a-new-manager) sets out.
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Keep the Hiring Plan

1. On **People**, add each planned hire with its hiring state, as [People](people.md#add-a-person-or-a-planned-hire) sets out.
1. Change its **State** as the hire is approved, and select **Publish** in the top bar.
1. Once that change is approved and someone is hired, fill the planned hire with them, as [People](people.md#fill-a-planned-hire) sets out, and select **Publish**.

## Keep the Lists Current

1. On **Lists**, add choices as they're needed, as [Lists](lists.md#add-choices) sets out.
1. Retire a choice no longer used, as [Lists](lists.md#retire-or-remove-a-choice) sets out.
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Keep the Feature Teams Current

1. On **Feature assignments**, choose **Teams**, and move people between teams from the month they move, as [Feature assignments](feature-assignments.md#put-someone-on-a-feature-team-from-a-month) sets out.
1. Add, rename or remove teams on the **Org chart**, as [Org chart](org-chart.md#change-a-feature-team) sets out.
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Decide Who Sees What

On **Data classes**, under **Administration**, change the class of a detail, column or list, as [Data classes](data-classes.md#change-a-class) sets out.
