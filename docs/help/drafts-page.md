# Drafts page

The **Drafts** page, under **People** in the left-hand menu, lists the drafts, newest first. A draft is a change that waits for approval before it goes live. The page shows the drafts waiting for approval, the ones rejected, and your own changes you haven't published yet.

Approvers approve or reject drafts here. Everyone who sees the plan, features, people or configuration sees the drafts of what they see.

See [Drafts](drafts.md) for what a draft's marks mean, and what happens to it.

## Main Tasks

### Find a Draft

Type in **Search drafts**: a name, a value, or who made the draft. To find the rejected drafts, type `Rejected`.

### Approve Drafts

1. Tick each draft you want to approve. To tick every draft shown that you can approve, select **Select all waiting**.
1. Select the **Approve** button. It names how many you ticked, such as **Approve 3 drafts…**.
1. Check the dialog. It lists any other drafts these depend on, which are approved with them.
1. Select **Approve**.

You're done when the drafts leave the list. They're live straight away.

### Reject Drafts

1. Tick each draft you want to reject.
1. Select **Reject…**.
1. Type the **Reason**.
1. Select **Reject**.

Drafts that use the ones you rejected are rejected with them. Each one goes back to whoever made it, with your reason.

You're done when the drafts show a red dot in a ring, with your reason under them.

## Marks and Terms

- **An entry** says who made the draft and what it does, followed by each value it changes. It reads, for example, **drafted adding**, **drafted removing**, **drafted changes to** or **drafted a new order for**. When several people changed one record, each value names who changed it.
- **Reason:**, under an entry, after a red dot in a ring, says why the draft was rejected.
- **Waiting for a rejected draft to be resubmitted** means the draft depends on one that was rejected.
- **Tick boxes** show only if your role approves that kind of draft.
- **A greyed-out tick box** is on a rejected draft, a draft waiting on one, your own unpublished change, or a record someone else is changing.
- **A draft you changed** needs another approver. If you tick one, a line under the buttons says so, and you can't select **Approve**. A role with **Approve own drafts**, such as the Engineering Director, can approve its own.
- **Another approver needed** opens when your role can't decide every draft involved. When you approve, that's the drafts you ticked and those they depend on. When you reject, it's the drafts you ticked and those that use them. It also opens when you approve drafts that depend on one you changed yourself. Nothing is approved or rejected.

## Where Changes Go

Approved drafts go live at once. Both approving and rejecting are recorded in the [change history](change-history.md).
