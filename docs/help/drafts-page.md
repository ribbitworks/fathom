# Drafts page

The **Drafts** page, under **People**, lists the drafts, newest first: those waiting for approval, those rejected, and the account's own unpublished changes. Approvers approve or reject them here. Everyone who sees the plan, features, people or configuration sees the drafts of what they see. What a draft's marks mean, and what happens to it, are in [Drafts](drafts.md), also under **Help** in the left-hand menu.

## Main Tasks

### Find a Draft

Type in the search box, such as a name, a value or who made the draft. Searching for `Rejected` finds the rejected drafts.

### Approve Drafts

1. Tick each draft to approve, or **Select all waiting** to tick every draft shown that can be approved.
1. Select the button that reads **Approve** and the number ticked, such as **Approve 3 drafts…**.
1. Check the dialog, which lists any other drafts these depend on, approved with them.
1. Select **Approve**.

### Reject Drafts

1. Tick each draft to reject.
1. Select **Reject…**.
1. Type the **Reason**, and select **Reject**.

Drafts that use the rejected ones are rejected with them. What happens to a rejected draft is in [Drafts](drafts.md#what-happens-to-a-draft).

## Marks and Terms

- **An entry** says who made the draft and what it does, such as **drafted adding**, **drafted removing**, **drafted changes to** or **drafted a new order for**, followed by each value it changes. When several people changed one record, each value names who changed it.
- **Reason:** under an entry, after a red dot in a ring, gives why the draft was rejected.
- **Waiting for a rejected draft to be resubmitted** means the draft depends on one that was rejected.
- **Tick boxes** show only to roles that approve that kind of draft. A box is greyed out on a rejected draft, a draft waiting on one, the account's own unpublished change, and a record someone else is changing.
- **A draft this account changed** needs another approver, and a line under the buttons says so. The role's **Approve own drafts**, held by the Engineering Director, lets it approve its own.
- **Another approver needed** opens when the drafts, or those they depend on, include a kind the role doesn't approve, or drafts this account changed. Nothing is decided.

## Where Changes Go

Approved drafts go live at once, and both approving and rejecting are recorded in the [change history](change-history.md).
