# Working as a Project Manager

As a Project Manager, you see projects through to delivery: their milestones, the features they need, and the people and teams working on them.

## What to Know First

- **What you see:** you see people with their Personal and Confidential data, and the hiring plan, which holds your organization's planned hires.
- **What you approve:** you approve other people's drafts of the plan, features and releases, allocations, people and planned hires, feature teams, lists, and the planning settings. Your own drafts need another approver, such as the Engineering Director.
- **Milestones:** a project's dated points, such as a delivery to the customer. **Next delivery** is the next milestone marked **Customer delivery**.
- **Drafts:** your changes are drafts. Nobody else sees them until you select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Keep a Project on Track

1. On **Projects**, open the project's details. See [Projects](projects.md#see-a-records-details).
1. Add or change its milestones. **Next delivery** follows from them. See [Projects](projects.md#add-a-milestone-to-a-project).
1. Read its **Scope history** to see each feature added or removed, and when. See [Projects](projects.md#see-how-a-projects-or-proposals-scope-changed).
1. To see where each feature it needs stands, select **Show features** in the project's **⋯** menu. See [Projects](projects.md#go-from-a-project-to-its-milestones-or-features).
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## See Who Works on What

1. Select the **Feature assignments** page to see who is on which feature team each month. See [Feature assignments](feature-assignments.md#read-the-table).
1. Select the **Capacity** page, and choose **People**, to see what each person works on. See [Capacity](capacity.md#read-who-works-on-what).
1. To see each team's people together, group by **Feature team**. See [Capacity](capacity.md#group-the-rows).

## Keep a View

A view is how you've set up a table, such as its search and filters. You can save one on each **Projects** tab, such as **Milestones**, to come back to. Only you see it. See [Saved views](saved-views.md#save-a-view).

## Approve Others' Drafts

1. Select the **Drafts** page.
1. Approve or reject the drafts waiting. See [Drafts page](drafts-page.md#approve-drafts).

You're done when your projects' milestones are current, and no drafts are waiting for you.
