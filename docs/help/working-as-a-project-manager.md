# Working as a Project Manager

As a Project Manager, you see projects through to delivery: their milestones, the features they need, the releases that ship them, and the people and teams working on them. Your pages are **Projects**, **Features** and **Releases**, under **Plan** in the left-hand menu, **Capacity**, under **People**, and **Drafts**, under **History**.

## What to Know First

- **What you see:** you see people with their Personal and Confidential data, and the hiring plan, which holds the planned hires.
- **What you approve:** you approve other people's drafts of the plan, features and releases, schedules, allocations, people and planned hires, feature teams, lists, the columns on People, Features and Activities, and the planning settings. Your own drafts need another approver, such as the Engineering Director.
- **Milestones:** a project's dated points, such as a delivery to the customer. **Next delivery** is the next milestone marked **Customer delivery**.
- **Cycles and releases:** a feature targets a cycle, a planning period such as a quarter. A release ships the features that target its cycles.
- **Drafts:** your changes are drafts. Nobody else sees them until you select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Keep Projects and Proposals Current

Once you've made your changes, select **Publish** in the top bar.

### Add Customers, Proposals and Projects

1. On **Projects**, select the **Customers**, **Proposals** or **Projects** tab.
1. Select **New customer**, **New proposal** or **New project**.
1. Fill in the form, and select **Add**.

See [Projects](projects.md#add-a-project-proposal-customer-or-milestone).

### Change a Proposal's Status

As a proposal is won, lost or withdrawn, choose its new **Status** on the **Proposals** tab. See [Projects](projects.md#marks-and-terms).

### Add or Change Milestones

1. On **Projects**, open the project's details.
1. Select **Add milestone**, or **Edit…** in a milestone's **⋯** menu to change one.
1. Type its **Name**.
1. Type its **Date**.
1. If the customer receives something then, tick **Customer delivery**.
1. Select **Add milestone** or **Save**.

**Next delivery** follows from the milestones. See [Projects](projects.md#add-a-milestone-to-a-project).

## Tie Features to Projects and Proposals

Once you've made your changes, select **Publish** in the top bar.

### Choose the Features a Project Needs

1. On **Projects**, select the project's or proposal's **Features** cell, and press Enter.
1. Pick each feature it needs.
1. Press Enter again to finish.

See [Projects](projects.md#choose-the-features-a-project-needs).

### Read the Scope History

Open the project's details. **Scope history**, at the end, lists each feature added or removed, and when, newest first. See [Projects](projects.md#see-how-a-projects-or-proposals-scope-changed).

### See Where Each Feature Stands

Select **Show on Features** in the project's **⋯** menu. **Features** opens, showing only the features it needs, with their status and target cycle. See [Projects](projects.md#go-from-a-project-to-its-milestones-features-or-activities).

## Move Work When Dates Change

Once you've made your changes, select **Publish** in the top bar.

### Move a Feature to Another Cycle

On **Features**, choose the new cycle in the feature's **Target cycle**. **Release** then names the release that covers it. See [Features](features.md#plan-when-a-feature-ships).

### Move a Milestone

1. On **Projects**, open the project's details.
1. Select **Edit…** in the milestone's **⋯** menu.
1. Type its new **Date**.
1. Select **Save**.

See [Projects](projects.md#add-a-milestone-to-a-project).

## Ship Features in Releases

Once you've made your changes, select **Publish** in the top bar.

### Make a Release

1. On **Releases**, select **New release**.
1. Choose its **Schedule**.
1. Type its **Name**.
1. Choose its **First cycle**.
1. Choose its **Last cycle**.
1. Select **Make release**.

See [Releases](releases.md#make-a-release).

### Write What a Release Ships

1. Select the **⋯** at the end of the release's row.
1. Select **Add note…**.
1. Write the note.
1. Select **Save**.

See [Releases](releases.md#write-a-releases-note).

### See What a Release Holds

Open the release. Its **Features** row lists the features that target its cycles, and **Show on Features** opens them on **Features**. See [Releases](releases.md#follow-a-record-to-another).

## See Who Works on What

### See Who Is on Each Feature Team

On **Capacity**, choose **Feature teams**. It shows who is on which feature team, month by month. See [Capacity](capacity.md#move-through-the-months).

### See What Each Person Works On

On **Capacity**, choose **Allocations**. Each month shows the features each person works on, and how much of their month each takes. See [Capacity](capacity.md#in-allocations).

### Group by Feature Team

On **Capacity**, choose **Allocations**, then select **Group by** and **Feature team**, to see each team's people together. See [Capacity](capacity.md#group-the-rows).

## Approve or Reject Drafts

You approve or reject drafts in every area your role approves, such as projects, features, releases, allocations and people. Drafts you changed yourself stay waiting for another approver.

### Approve on the Drafts Page

1. Select the **Drafts** page, under **History**, then **To decide**.
1. Tick each draft you want to approve.
1. Select **Approve** at the right of the page's heading. It names the count, such as **Approve 3 drafts…**.
1. Check the dialog, which names any other drafts approved with them.
1. Select **Approve**.

See [Drafts page](drafts-page.md#approve-drafts).

### Check What a Draft Holds

1. Point at the draft's row.
1. Select the details button: the small box at the right of its **Record** cell.

The details show what the draft changes, and the other drafts that approving or rejecting it takes along. See [Drafts page](drafts-page.md#see-a-drafts-details).

### Reject a Draft With a Reason

1. On **To decide**, tick each draft you want to reject.
1. Select **Reject…** at the right of the page's heading.
1. Type the **Reason**.
1. Select **Reject**.

Each draft goes back to whoever made it, with your reason. See [Drafts page](drafts-page.md#reject-drafts).

You're done when your projects' milestones and features are current, your releases hold what they ship, and no drafts are waiting for you.
