# Working as a Feature Lead

A Feature Lead leads a feature team: they keep its features and estimates current, and shape who is on the team. Their changes are drafts: a Product Manager, a Project Manager or the Engineering Director approves changes to features, and a Project Manager or the Engineering Director changes to the team.

## Keep the Team's Features Current

1. On **Features**, change each of the team's features' **Status** as the work moves along, as [Features](features.md#change-a-feature) sets out.
1. Give or update each estimate in **Effort**, as [Features](features.md#give-an-estimate) sets out.
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## See What a Cycle or Release Holds

On **Features**, group by **Cycle** or **Release**, as [Features](features.md#group-the-table) sets out.

## Shape the Feature Team

1. On the **Org chart**, choose **Feature teams**, and open the panel of the team to change, as [Org chart](org-chart.md#change-a-feature-team) sets out.
1. Change its members or its lead.
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Find Someone on the Org Chart

Type a name in **Find a person or team** on the **Org chart**, as [Org chart](org-chart.md#find-a-person-or-team) sets out.
