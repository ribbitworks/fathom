# Private pages

A private page is a table of your own, under **Mine** in the left-hand menu. You type in it as you would in a spreadsheet. Nobody else sees it, an Administrator included.

## What to Know First

- **Who has them:** your role needs **Make private pages**. Without it, **Mine** isn't in the left-hand menu. See [Roles](roles.md).
- **The violet ring:** a small violet ring before a page's name, in the left-hand menu and the page's heading, says the page is private.
- **Columns:** each page has its own columns. The first one names each row, so every row needs a value in it.
- **No drafts:** whatever you type is saved at once. Nothing waits for approval, and nothing joins your unpublished changes.
- **Lists:** a **Choice from a list** column offers the choices of a list on the [Lists](lists.md) page.

## Main Tasks

### Make a Page

1. Under **Mine**, select **New page**.
1. Type the page's **Name**, and select **Next**.
1. Under **Columns**, the first row is **Name**, which names each row of the page. Change its name or its **Type** if you like: it can be **Text**, **Number** or **Date**.
1. Type a new column's name in the empty row at the end. Another empty row shows under it.
1. Choose the column's **Type**. For **Choice from a list**, also choose its **List**.
1. Press Enter to go to the next row, and add more columns the same way. To take one away, select the **×** at the end of its row.
1. Select **Add page**.

**Back** returns to the page's name, keeping the columns. Each page needs its own name, and each column a name of its own on the page.

You're done when the page opens, and shows last under **Mine**.

### Add and Change Rows

1. Open the page from **Mine**.
1. Type the first column's value in the empty row at the end of the table, and press Enter.

   The row is also saved when you Tab out of its last cell, or select anywhere outside it. Until it's saved, a violet ring before it says it's new.
1. Select a cell you want to change, start typing or press Enter, and press Enter again to keep it.

Tab moves to the next cell, and the arrow keys move around the table. Space ticks a **Checkbox** cell. You can paste rows from a spreadsheet too.

To undo your last change, press Cmd+Z on a Mac, or Ctrl+Z elsewhere.

You're done when the row shows in the table with the values you typed.

### Remove a Row

1. Point at the row, and select the **⋯** at the right of its first cell. You can also right-click the row.
1. Select **Remove row**.

The row is removed at once. A notification says so, with **Undo** to bring it back.

You're done when the row is gone from the table.

### Remove a Column

1. Select a column's heading, other than the first.
1. Select **Remove column…**, then **Remove**.

The column and its values are removed. A notification says so, with **Undo** to bring them back, and Cmd+Z or Ctrl+Z undoes it too.

You're done when the column is gone from the table.

### Rename or Remove a Page

1. Select the **⋯** at the right of the page's heading.
1. Select **Rename page…**, type its new name, and select **Rename**. Or select **Remove page…**, then **Remove**.

A removed page goes with its rows, and the next page under **Mine** opens. A notification says so, with **Undo** to bring the page back. **Copy link** and **Copy key** in the same menu copy the page's link and key.

You're done when the page shows its new name, or is gone from **Mine**.

### Find, Group and Export Rows

The bar above the table works as on every table:

- **Search:** type in it to find the rows holding what you typed.
- **Metrics:** counts the rows, broken down by a column.
- **Group by:** puts the rows under a heading for each choice of a **Choice from a list** column. It shows once the page has one.
- **Views:** shows or hides columns, such as **Key**.

The Export icon at the top right saves what the table shows as a CSV or TSV file.

You're done when the table shows the rows you wanted, or the file is saved.
