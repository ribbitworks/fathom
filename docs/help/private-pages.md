# Private pages

A private page is a table of your own, under **Mine** in the left-hand menu. You type in it as you would in a spreadsheet. Nobody else sees it, an Administrator included.

## What to Know First

- **Who has them:** your role needs **Make private pages**. Without it, **Mine** isn't in the left-hand menu. If your role loses it, your pages are hidden until it comes back. See [Roles](roles.md).
- **The violet ring:** a small violet ring before a page's name, in the left-hand menu and the page's heading, says the page is private.
- **Columns:** each page has its own columns. The first one names each row, so every row needs a value in it.
- **Set once:** once you add a page, its name and columns stay as you made them. So choose them with care.
- **No drafts:** whatever you type is saved at once. Most other changes in Fathom are drafts, which wait for approval; see [Drafts](drafts.md). Nothing here does.
- **Lists:** a **Choice from a list** column offers the choices of a list that already exists. If the list you need isn't there, ask whoever manages lists to make it. See [Lists](lists.md).
- **Keys:** each row has a key, such as **#ROW-4X9T**, and **Copy link** in its menu. A link to a row opens the page, and only for you. See [Keys and links](keys-and-links.md).

## Main Tasks

### Make a Page

1. Under **Mine**, select **New page**.
1. Type the page's **Name**.
1. Select **Next**. A table of the page's columns shows, headed **Name** and **Type**.
1. In its first row, change the column's name or its **Type** if you like. This column names each row of the page, so it can be **Text**, **Number** or **Date**.
1. Type a new column's name in the empty row at the end. Another empty row shows under it.
1. Choose the column's **Type**.
1. If you chose **Choice from a list**, choose the list under **List**, which shows once a column needs one.
1. Press Enter to go to the next row, and add the next column the same way.
1. Select **Add page**.

To take a column away before you add the page, select the **×** at the end of its row. **Back** returns to the page's name, keeping the columns. Each page needs its own name, and each column a name of its own on the page.

You're done when the page opens, and shows last under **Mine**.

### Add and Change Rows

1. Open the page from **Mine**.
1. Type the first column's value in the empty row at the end of the table, and press Enter.

   The row is also saved when you Tab out of its last cell, or select anywhere outside it. Until it's saved, a violet ring before it says it's new.
1. To change a value, select its cell.
1. Start typing, or press Enter to change what's there.
1. Press Enter to keep the change.

Tab moves to the next cell, and the arrow keys move around the table. Space ticks a **Checkbox** cell. You can paste rows from a spreadsheet too.

To undo your last change, press Cmd+Z on a Mac, or Ctrl+Z elsewhere.

You're done when the row shows in the table with the values you typed.

### Remove a Row

1. Point at the row, and select the **⋯** at the right of its first cell, or right-click the row.
1. Select **Remove row**.

The row is removed at once. A notification says so, with **Undo** to bring it back.

You're done when the row is gone from the table.

### Find, Group and Export Rows

The bar above the table works as on every table:

- **Search:** type in it to find the rows holding what you typed.
- **Metrics:** counts the rows, broken down by a column.
- **Group by:** puts the rows under a heading for each choice of a **Choice from a list** column. It shows once the page has one.
- **Views:** shows or hides columns, such as **Key**. A view here isn't saved for later.

To export what the table shows:

1. Select the **Export** button at the right of the page's heading. Its icon is an arrow pointing down to a line.
1. Choose **CSV file** or **TSV file**.
1. Choose where to save it, if your browser asks.

Your own changes to your pages show in your change history, and nobody else's. See [Change history](change-history.md).

You're done when the table shows the rows you wanted, or the file is saved.
