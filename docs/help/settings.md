# Settings

**Settings** holds Fathom's settings, which apply to everyone: its time zone, and the rules for passwords. Each has its own panel. You find it under **Administration** in the left-hand menu.

Only a role with **Edit** on **Administration**, such as the Administrator, changes them. The Engineering Director and the Executive see the settings without **Save**.

## What to Know First

- **Time zone:** Fathom's time zone decides which day is today, for everyone. That's the day a change takes effect when none is given, and it decides who still works here. Times on screen still follow each person's own settings.
- **Password requirements:** the rules for the passwords people choose.
- **Generated passwords:** the passwords Fathom makes itself, such as for a new account.
- **No drafts:** a change here takes effect straight away, for everyone, without approval. Each change is recorded in the access history.

See [Access history](access-history.md) for more.

## Main Tasks

### Change the Time Zone

1. In **Time zone**, type part of the time zone's name, such as `Vancouver`.
1. Choose the time zone.
1. Check the day and time shown under it.
1. Select **Save**.

You're done when **Save** is greyed out again, and the day and time under **Time zone** are right.

### Set the Rules for Passwords

1. Under **Password requirements**, set the **Minimum length**.
1. Tick what each password **Must have**.
1. Under **Generated passwords**, set the **Length**.
1. Tick what generated passwords **Use**.
1. Tick **Leave out characters that look alike**, if you want them left out.
1. Select **Save**.

A tighter rule applies the next time each person changes their password.

If the generated passwords would break the requirements, a red box says why, and you can't select **Save** until you fix it.

You're done when **Save** is greyed out again, with your new rules showing.

## Marks and Terms

- **Common or easily guessed passwords** are always refused, whatever the settings say.
