# Working as an Engineering Manager

As an Engineering Manager, you staff the work: what the features will take, and who is on which feature team, month by month. You see everyone's Personal and Confidential data, and the hiring plan, which holds the planned hires. Your pages are **Features**, under **Plan** in the left-hand menu, and **Capacity** and the **Org chart**, under **People**.

## What to Know First

- **Feature teams:** a feature team is a group that works on features together, whoever its members report to.
- **Allocations:** an allocation is the share of someone's month planned for one feature, such as 50%. **Capacity** shows them, read only.
- **Staffed:** a planned or in-progress feature is staffed once someone is allocated to it.
- **Drafts:** your changes are drafts. Nobody else sees them until you select **Publish** in the top bar. A Project Manager or the Engineering Director approves your changes to teams.

## Estimate the Features

### Give an Estimate

1. On **Features**, type each feature's estimate in **Effort**, in the unit the column shows, such as person-days.
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

See [Features](features.md#give-an-estimate).

### See What Each Cycle Holds

Select **Group by**, then **Cycle**. Each cycle's features show under its heading, and **Later** holds those that target no cycle yet. See [Features](features.md#group-the-table).

### Find Features Not Staffed

1. Open the **Staffed** column's menu, and select **Filter…**.
1. Pick **No**. These are the planned or in-progress features nobody is allocated to.
1. Where **Feature team** reads **No team** in red, choose the team that builds the feature.
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

See [Features](features.md#give-a-feature-to-a-team).

## Staff the Feature Teams

Once you've made your changes, select **Publish** in the top bar.

### Put Someone on a Team From a Month

1. On **Capacity**, in **Feature teams**, select the person's cell in the month they start.
1. Press Enter. The team list opens.
1. Type part of the team's name, and press Tab.

To take someone off a team from a month on, select their cell in that month, and press Delete. See [Capacity](capacity.md#put-someone-on-a-feature-team-from-a-month).

### Change a Team's Lead or Several Members

1. On the **Org chart**, choose **Feature teams** in the switch.
1. Select the **Feature teams** button, and choose the team. Its panel opens beside the chart.
1. Point at **Feature lead** or **Members**, and select the pencil at its right.
1. Make the change.

See [Org chart](org-chart.md#change-a-feature-team).

## Check Who Is Over or Under Allocated

### Group by Feature Team

On **Capacity**, choose **Allocations**, then select **Group by** and **Feature team**. Each team ends with a total row. See [Capacity](capacity.md#group-the-rows).

### Show Only Who Is Over or Under

Select **Views**, then **Over** or **Under**, or both. They keep the people who are **Over**, or **Under** or **Not allocated**, in the current month or a later one. See [Capacity](capacity.md#find-people).

## Look at the Teams

### Find a Team

On the **Org chart**, type part of the team's name in **Find a person or team**, and choose the team. See [Org chart](org-chart.md#find-a-person-or-team).

### Show One Team Alone

1. On the manager's card, select **Show only this team**: the small org chart button at the card's top right.
1. To see everyone again, select **Show everyone**.

See [Org chart](org-chart.md#show-one-team-alone).

### See an Earlier Date

1. In the date row under **Find a person or team**, type a day in **Date**, or select an arrow to move a month.
1. To come back to today, select **Today**.

See [Org chart](org-chart.md#see-another-date).

You're done when each feature has an estimate and a team, each team has the people it needs, and you know who is over or under allocated.
