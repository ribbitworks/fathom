# Working as an Engineering Manager

As an Engineering Manager, you staff the work: who is on which feature team, month by month, and what the features they build will take. You see everyone's Personal and Confidential data, and the hiring plan, which holds the planned hires.

## What to Know First

- **Feature teams:** a feature team is a group that works on features together, whoever its members report to.
- **Allocations:** an allocation is the share of someone's month planned for one feature, such as 50%.
- **Drafts:** your changes are drafts. Nobody else sees them until you select **Publish** in the top bar. A Project Manager or the Engineering Director approves your changes to teams.

## Staff a Feature Team

1. On **Feature assignments**, put each person on the team from the month they start. See [Feature assignments](feature-assignments.md#put-someone-on-a-feature-team-from-a-month).
1. To change a team's lead, or several members at once, use the team's panel on the **Org chart**. See [Org chart](org-chart.md#change-a-feature-team).
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Check Who Is Over or Under Allocated

1. On **Capacity**, choose **People**.
1. Group by **Feature team**. See [Capacity](capacity.md#group-the-rows).
1. Look for **Over** and **Under** in each month. See [Capacity](capacity.md#in-people).

## Estimate Features

1. On **Features**, type each feature's estimate in **Effort**. See [Features](features.md#give-an-estimate).
1. To see what each cycle holds, group by **Cycle**. See [Features](features.md#group-the-table).
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## See a Team on the Org Chart

1. On the **Org chart**, find the team. See [Org chart](org-chart.md#find-a-person-or-team).
1. Show the team alone. See [Org chart](org-chart.md#show-one-team-alone).
1. To see the team as it was on an earlier date, choose the date. See [Org chart](org-chart.md#see-another-date).

## Count the People

On **People**, select **Metrics**, and break the rows down by a column, such as level or site. See [People](people.md#count-people).

You're done when each team has the people it needs, and you know who is over or under allocated.
