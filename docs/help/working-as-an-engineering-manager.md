# Working as an Engineering Manager

An Engineering Manager staffs the work: who is on which feature team, month by month, and what the features they build will take. Engineering Managers see everyone's Personal details and the hiring plan, and their changes to teams are drafts that a Project Manager or the Engineering Director approves.

## Staff a Feature Team

1. Select the **Feature assignments** page.
1. Put each person on the team from the month they start, as [Feature assignments](feature-assignments.md#put-someone-on-a-feature-team-from-a-month) sets out.
1. To change a team's lead or several members at once, use the team's panel on the **Org chart**, as [Org chart](org-chart.md#change-a-feature-team) sets out.
1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.

## Check Who Is Over or Under Allocated

1. On **Capacity**, choose **People**.
1. Group by **Feature team**, as [Capacity](capacity.md#group-the-rows) sets out.
1. Look for **Over** and **Under** in each month, as [Capacity](capacity.md#in-people) explains.

## Estimate Features

1. On **Features**, type each feature's **Effort**, as [Features](features.md#give-an-estimate) sets out.
1. Group by **Cycle** to see what each cycle holds, as [Features](features.md#group-the-table) sets out.

## See a Team on the Org Chart

1. On the **Org chart**, find the team, as [Org chart](org-chart.md#find-a-person-or-team) sets out.
1. Show the team alone, as [Org chart](org-chart.md#show-one-team-alone) sets out.
1. To see it as it was on an earlier date, choose the date, as [Org chart](org-chart.md#see-another-date) sets out.

## Count the People

On **People**, select **Metrics** and break the rows down by a column, such as level or site, as [People](people.md#count-people) sets out.
