# Pages

The left-hand menu shows only the pages your role lets you open. A page your role can't open is left out, and nothing says it's there. This page lists every page of Fathom. It says what each is for, where it is, and which permission shows it.

## What to Know First

- **Sections:** the left-hand menu groups the pages into sections: **Plan**, **People**, **Administration**, **History** and **Help**.
- **Permissions:** a role is a set of permissions. Each one is a box in the grid on the [Roles](roles.md) page. **View Plan**, for example, is the **View** box in the **Plan** row. **Edit** brings **View** with it, so a role that edits an area sees it too.
- **Personal details:** **View Personal details** is the **Personal** box under **Data classification**. Some pages need it, since they show the months people join and leave teams. Those dates are Personal unless your organization moves them. If it does, these pages need the class the dates are in instead. See [Data classes](data-classes.md).
- **Starting roles:** Fathom starts with nine roles, one for each kind of work. The tables below say which of them see each page. Your organization can change these roles and make others, so check a role's own boxes on the Roles page.

## Plan

## People

| Page | What it's for | Shown with | Starting roles that see it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [People](people.md) | It lists everyone in the organization and the planned hires, as a table. | **View People** | Every role but Administrator |
| [Org chart](org-chart.md) | It draws reporting teams and feature teams as cards, on any date. | **View People** | Every role but Administrator |
| [Feature assignments](feature-assignments.md) | It shows who is on which feature team, month by month. | **View Feature teams** with **View Personal details** | Project Manager, Engineering Manager, Engineering Director, Workforce Administrator, Executive |
| [Drafts](drafts-page.md) | It lists the drafts waiting. Approvers approve or reject them there. | Any of **View Plan**, **View Features and releases**, **View People** or **View Configuration** | Every role but Administrator |
| [Capacity](capacity.md) | It shows what each person works on, month by month, and the planning settings. | Its **People** mode needs **View Allocations**, **View Feature teams**, **View People** and **View Personal details**. Its **Settings** mode needs **View Configuration**. Either mode shows the page. | Product Manager, Project Manager, Engineering Manager, Engineering Director, Workforce Administrator, Executive |
| [Lists](lists.md) | It holds the lists of choices that columns offer. | **View Configuration** | Product Manager, Project Manager, Engineering Manager, Engineering Director, Workforce Administrator, Executive |

The Product Manager sees Capacity in its **Settings** mode only, since the role doesn't hold **View Personal details**.

## Help

Everyone sees the **Help** section, whatever their role.

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Roles** | It says what each role Fathom starts with is for. |
| [Drafts](drafts.md) | It says what the marks on a draft mean, and what happens to it. |
| **About Fathom** | It says which version of Fathom this is. It opens in a box over the page you're on. |

## Where Fathom Opens

Fathom opens on the **Overview**. If your role doesn't see the plan, it opens on the first page your role sees instead, in the order of the left-hand menu. The Administrator, for example, opens on **Accounts**. A role that sees no page opens on **Roles** under **Help**.

You're done when you've found the page you need, and know which permission shows it.
