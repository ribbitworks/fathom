# Pages

The left-hand menu shows only the pages your role lets you open. A page your role can't open is left out, and nothing says it's there.

This page lists every page of Fathom. It says what each is for, where it is, and which permission shows it.

## What to Know First

- **Sections:** the left-hand menu groups the pages into sections: **Mine**, **Plan**, **People**, **Administration**, **History** and **Help**.
- **Permissions:** a role is a set of permissions. Each one is a box in the grid on the [Roles](roles.md) page.
  - **View Plan**, for example, is the **View** box in the **Plan** row.
  - **Edit** brings **View** with it, so a role that edits an area sees it too.
- **Personal details:** **View Personal details** is the **Personal** box under **Data classification**, and **View Confidential data** is the **Confidential** box.
  - Some pages need **View Personal details**, since they show when people start and leave, and the months they join and leave teams.
  - Those dates are Personal unless someone moves them to another class. If they're moved, these pages need the class the dates are in instead. See [Data classes](data-classes.md).
- **Starting roles:** Fathom starts with nine roles, one for each kind of work. The lists below say which of them see each page. These roles can be changed, and others made, so check a role's own boxes on the Roles page.

## Mine

**Mine** comes first in the left-hand menu. It lists the pages you made yourself, each after a violet ring, then **New page**. Nobody else sees them. See [Private pages](private-pages.md).

- **Shown with:** **Make private pages**
- **Starting roles that see it:** Product Manager, Feature Lead, Project Manager, Engineering Manager, Engineering Director, Workforce Administrator

## Plan

- [Overview](overview.md): Fathom opens on it, with your role's jobs.
  - **Shown with:** no permission needed
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Every role
- [Projects](projects.md): It holds projects, proposals, customers and milestones.
  - **Shown with:** **View Plan**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Every role but Administrator
- [Features](features.md): It shows every feature planned, as a table or a board.
  - **Shown with:** **View Features and releases**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Every role but Administrator
- [Activities](activities.md): It shows the work releases and projects need that isn't a feature.
  - **Shown with:** **View Features and releases**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Every role but Administrator
- [Releases](releases.md): It holds schedules, their cycles and releases, and the timeline.
  - **Shown with:** **View Features and releases** or **View Configuration**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Every role but Administrator

## People

- [People](people.md): It lists everyone and the planned hires, as a table.
  - **Shown with:** **View People**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Every role but Administrator
- [Org chart](org-chart.md): It draws reporting teams and feature teams as cards, today or, with **View Personal details**, on past dates. Future dates in **Reporting teams** also need **View Confidential data**.
  - **Shown with:** **View People**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Every role but Administrator
- [Capacity](capacity.md): It shows who is on which feature team and what each person works on, month by month.
  - **Shown with:** One tab is enough to show the page. Each tab needs these permissions:
    - Its **Feature teams** tab needs **View Feature teams** and **View Personal details**.
    - Its **Allocations** tab needs **View Allocations**, **View Feature teams**, **View People** and **View Personal details**.
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Project Manager, Engineering Manager, Engineering Director, Workforce Administrator, Executive

The Product Manager doesn't see Capacity, since the role doesn't hold **View Personal details**.

## Administration

- [Accounts](accounts.md): It lists who can sign in, and the roles each holds.
  - **Shown with:** **View Administration**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Engineering Director, Executive, Administrator
- [Roles](roles.md): It shows what each role lets its holders see and do.
  - **Shown with:** **View Administration**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Engineering Director, Executive, Administrator
- [Application settings](settings.md): It holds the time zone and the rules for passwords.
  - **Shown with:** **View Administration**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Engineering Director, Executive, Administrator
- [Lists](lists.md): It holds the lists of choices that columns offer.
  - **Shown with:** **View Configuration**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Product Manager, Project Manager, Engineering Manager, Engineering Director, Workforce Administrator, Executive
- [Planning settings](planning-settings.md): It holds the working time, the working week, and the figures forecasts start from.
  - **Shown with:** **View Configuration**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Product Manager, Project Manager, Engineering Manager, Engineering Director, Workforce Administrator, Executive
- [Data classes](data-classes.md): It shows the class of each detail, column and list.
  - **Shown with:** **Classify data**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Engineering Director, Workforce Administrator

## History

- [Drafts](drafts-page.md): It lists the drafts in up to three tabs. Approvers approve or reject drafts under **To decide**, and you publish or discard some of your own changes under **Not published**.
  - **Shown with:** Any of **View Plan**, **View Features and releases**, **View People** or **View Configuration**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Every role but Administrator
- [Change history](change-history.md): It lists every change saved, who made it, and the values before and after.
  - **Shown with:** **View** on any area but **Administration**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Every role but Administrator
- [Access history](access-history.md): It lists sign-ins, changes to accounts, roles and settings, and who viewed or exported private details.
  - **Shown with:** **View Administration**
  - **Starting roles that see it:** Engineering Director, Executive, Administrator

## Help

Everyone sees the **Help** section, whatever their role.

- **Help pages:** It lists every Help page.
- **About Fathom:** It says which version of Fathom this is. It opens in a box over the page you're on.

## Where Fathom Opens

Fathom opens on the **Overview**, whatever your role. It lists your role's jobs, each linking to the page where you do it. See [Overview](overview.md).

You're done when you've found the page you need, and know which permission shows it.
