# Working as a Team Member

As a Team Member, you see the plan and people's General details, such as their names, positions and teams. You read; you don't change any records. General details are those not kept as Personal, for private and HR details, or Confidential, for business data not yet announced.

## What to Know First

- **Features and cycles:** a feature is a body of work one feature team delivers. It targets a cycle, a planning period such as a quarter.
- **Releases:** a release ships the features that target its cycles. The **Releases** page shows them on a timeline.
- **Dots:** a dot before a value or a row marks a change still waiting for approval.

## See What's Planned

### See What Each Cycle or Release Holds

1. On **Features**, select **Group by**.
1. Choose **Cycle** or **Release**. The features show under a heading for each.

See [Features](features.md#group-the-table).

### See When Each Release Ships

1. Open **Releases**. It opens on the **Releases** tab, whose table gives each release's **Ships on** day.
1. Look at the timeline above the table. A diamond under each release marks the day it ships.
1. To see a release's name and dates, point at it on the timeline.

See [Releases](releases.md#use-the-timeline).

## Look at a Project

### See a Project's Details

1. On **Projects**, point at the project's row.
1. Select the details button: the small box at the right of its **Name** cell. The details open beside the table.

See [Projects](projects.md#see-a-records-details).

### See a Project's Milestones

1. Open the project's **⋯** menu.
1. Select **Show milestones**. **Milestones** opens, showing only that project's, with their dates.

See [Projects](projects.md#go-from-a-project-to-its-milestones-or-features).

## Find Someone

### Find a Person or Team

1. On the **Org chart**, type part of their name in **Find a person or team**.
1. Choose them. The chart centres their card, with a blue ring around it.

See [Org chart](org-chart.md#find-a-person-or-team).

### See Someone's Details

1. Select the person's name on their card. Their details open beside the chart.
1. To see them on **People** instead, select **Show in People**, near the top of their details.

See [Org chart](org-chart.md#see-someones-details).

You're done when you've found what you came for.
