# Working as an Engineering Director

As an Engineering Director, you run the plan and people. You change and approve almost anything, you see all Personal and Confidential data, and you decide which class each detail belongs to. You also see accounts, roles, settings and the access history, but you don't change them.

**People** and the **Org chart** are under **People** in the left-hand menu, **Data classes** under **Administration**, and **Drafts** under **History**.

## What to Know First

- **Drafts:** most of your changes are drafts. Nobody else sees them until you select **Publish** in the top bar. Of the roles Fathom starts with, yours is the only one that can approve its own drafts.
- **Planned hires:** a planned hire is a job you plan to fill. Its **State** says where the hire stands, such as **Request to hire** or **Approved to hire**.
- **Classes:** each detail, column and list has a class, which decides who sees it: **General**, **Personal** or **Confidential**.

## Plan the Hiring

A planned hire goes through three rounds, each published and approved before the next.

### Request a Hire

1. On **People**, select **New person**.
1. In **State**, choose **Request to hire**.
1. Fill in the rest of the details, and select **Add**.
1. Select **Publish**, and approve the draft on the **Drafts** page.

See [People](people.md#add-a-person-or-a-planned-hire).

### Mark a Hire Approved

1. Once the hire is agreed, change its **State** on **People** to **Approved to hire**.
1. Select **Publish**, and approve the draft.

See [People](people.md#change-a-person).

### Fill a Hire

1. Once someone is hired, select **Fill** in the planned hire's **Name** cell.
1. Choose **New person**, or **Existing person** for someone already here.
1. Fill in the form, and select **Fill** under it.
1. Select **Publish**, and approve the draft.

If the hire has allocations, remove them first, since Fathom doesn't move them to the person. See [People](people.md#fill-a-planned-hire).

## Keep People Current

### Give Someone a New Manager

1. If the new manager hasn't managed before, tick their **Manager?** on **People**.
1. Publish that, and approve it, in a round of its own.
1. On the person's row, choose their new **Manager**.
1. If the change took effect before today, type that day in **Manager assigned**.
1. Select **Publish**, and approve the draft.

See [People](people.md#give-someone-a-new-manager).

### Record Someone Leaving

1. On **People**, type the day they leave in the person's **End** cell. Don't remove them, so their row stays.
1. Select **Publish**, and approve the draft.

Once that day has passed, they show as having left. See [People](people.md#remove-a-person-or-planned-hire).

### See Another Date

1. Select the **Org chart** page.
1. Type a day in **Date**, or select an arrow to move a month.
1. Type a name in **Find a person or team**.
1. Choose the person, and check they're under the right manager.
1. To come back to today, select **Today**.

On an earlier day, you can also change someone's manager from that day. See [Org chart](org-chart.md#see-another-date).

## Choose Who Sees Personal and Confidential Data

A change of class takes effect straight away, with no draft and no **Undo**. It's recorded in the access history.

### See What Each Class Holds

1. Select the **Data classes** page.
1. Read each row's class, and its **About**, which says where the data shows.

The page has sections for **Built-in details**, **Columns** and **Lists**. See [Data classes](data-classes.md#see-what-each-class-holds).

### Change a Class

1. On **Data classes**, select **Change class…** in the row.
1. Choose the class.
1. Select **Change**.

To go back, change the class again. See [Data classes](data-classes.md#change-a-class).

## Approve or Reject Drafts

You approve or reject drafts in every area your role approves, your own included.

### Approve on the Drafts Page

1. Select the **Drafts** page, under **History**, then **To decide**.
1. Tick each draft you want to approve.
1. Select **Approve** at the right of the page's heading. It names the count, such as **Approve 3 drafts…**.
1. Check the dialog, which names any other drafts approved with them.
1. Select **Approve**.

See [Drafts page](drafts-page.md#approve-drafts).

### Approve Own Drafts

Other roles need someone else to approve their own drafts. Yours approves its own, since it holds **Approve own drafts**.

1. Select **Publish** in the top bar.
1. On **Drafts**, select **To decide**. Your drafts are there with the rest.
1. Tick them, then approve them as for any other draft.

See [Drafts page](drafts-page.md#marks-and-terms).

### Reject a Draft With a Reason

1. On **To decide**, tick each draft you want to reject.
1. Select **Reject…** at the right of the page's heading.
1. Type the **Reason**.
1. Select **Reject**.

Each draft goes back to whoever made it, with your reason. See [Drafts page](drafts-page.md#reject-drafts).

You're done when the plan and people show what you approved, and no drafts are waiting.
