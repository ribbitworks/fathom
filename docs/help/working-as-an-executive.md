# Working as an Executive

An Executive sees the whole plan and organization, with every person's Personal and Confidential details, and changes no records. They also see accounts, roles, settings, and both histories.

## Read the Plan

1. On **Features**, group by **Release** to see what each release ships, as [Features](features.md#group-the-table) sets out.
1. On **Releases**, use the timeline to see when each release ships, as [Releases](releases.md#use-the-timeline) sets out.
1. To see only what's approved, choose **Live only** in the **Draft** menu above the table, as [Drafts](drafts.md#what-the-draft-menu-shows) explains.

## Look at the Organization

1. On the **Org chart**, find a person or team, as [Org chart](org-chart.md#find-a-person-or-team) sets out.
1. Choose a date to see the organization then, as [Org chart](org-chart.md#see-another-date) sets out.
1. On **People**, select **Metrics** to count people by a column, such as level or site, as [People](people.md#count-people) sets out.

## Follow What Changed

1. Select the **Change history** page, and search for a person, a value or who made the change, as [Change history](change-history.md#find-a-change) sets out.
1. Select the **Access history** page to see who changed an account, a role or a setting.

## Keep a View

Save a view of **People** to come back to, as [Saved views](saved-views.md#save-a-view) sets out.
