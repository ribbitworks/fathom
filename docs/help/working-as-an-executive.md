# Working as an Executive

As an Executive, you see the whole plan and everyone in it, with every person's Personal and Confidential details. You read; you don't change any records. You also see who is on each feature team and what each person works on, on **Capacity**, and the accounts, roles, settings and both histories.

## What to Know First

- **Drafts:** changes waiting for approval are drafts, marked with dots. Each table's **Draft** menu chooses whether you see them.
- **Feature teams:** a feature team is a group that works on features together, whoever its members report to.
- **Allocations:** an allocation is the share of someone's month planned for one feature, such as 50%.

## Read the Plan

### See What Each Release Ships

1. On **Features**, select **Group by**.
1. Choose **Release**. The features show under a heading for each release.

See [Features](features.md#group-the-table).

### See When Each Release Ships

1. Open **Releases**. It opens on the **Releases** tab, whose table gives each release's **Ships on** day.
1. On the timeline above the table, a diamond under each release marks the day it ships.
1. To see a release's name and dates, point at it on the timeline.

See [Releases](releases.md#use-the-timeline).

### See Only What's Approved

1. Open the **Draft** menu above the table.
1. Choose **Live only**. A green dot marks each record or value a waiting draft would change.

See [Drafts](drafts.md#what-the-draft-menu-shows).

## See Who Works on What

### See Who Is on Each Feature Team

1. On **Capacity**, select **Feature teams** beside the heading. Each row is a person, and each column a month.
1. Select **Group by**.
1. Choose **Feature team**. Each team's members show under its name.

See [Capacity](capacity.md#group-the-rows).

### See What Each Person Works On

1. On **Capacity**, select **Allocations** beside the heading.
1. Type the person's name in the search box.
1. Read each month's cell. Each line names a feature and the share of the month it takes, such as **50%**.

**Over**, **Under** or **Not allocated**, in red, ends a month whose allocations don't add up to 100%. See [Capacity](capacity.md#in-allocations).

## Look at People

### Find a Person or Team

1. On the **Org chart**, type part of a name in **Find a person or team**.
1. Choose the person or team. The chart centres the card, with a blue ring around it.

See [Org chart](org-chart.md#find-a-person-or-team).

### See Another Date

1. On the **Org chart**, type a day in **Date**, under **Find a person or team**. You can also select an arrow to move a month, or drag the timeline.
1. To come back to today, select **Today**.

See [Org chart](org-chart.md#see-another-date).

### Count People by a Column

1. On **People**, select **Metrics**. The **Metrics** panel opens beside the table.
1. Under **Break down by**, choose the column to count by, such as level or site.

A search or a filter changes the counts. See [People](people.md#count-people).

## Follow What Changed

### Find a Change

1. On **Change history**, type in the **Search** box: a person's name, a value, or who made the change.
1. To see older entries, select **Show more** at the end of the page.

See [Change history](change-history.md#find-a-change).

### See Who Changed Access

1. On **Access history**, type a name, an email address or a word in the **Search** box.
1. Look for the entries ending in a grey **Worth checking** box. They mark changes that could give someone access or take it away.

See [Access history](access-history.md#check-the-marked-entries).

### Look at Accounts, Roles and Settings

These pages are under **Administration**. You see them, but you can't change them.

1. On **Accounts**, see each account's **Roles** and **Status**.
1. On **Roles**, select **View** in a role's row to see what it lets its holders see and do.
1. On **Settings**, see the time zone and the rules for passwords.

See [Roles](roles.md#see-what-a-role-gives).

You're done when you've found what you came for.
