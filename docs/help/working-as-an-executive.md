# Working as an Executive

As an Executive, you see the whole plan and everyone in it, with every person's Personal and Confidential details. You read; you don't change any records. You also see accounts, roles, settings, and both histories.

## What to Know First

- **Drafts:** changes waiting for approval are drafts, marked with dots. Each table's **Draft** menu chooses whether you see them.

## Read the Plan

1. On **Features**, group by **Release** to see what each release ships. See [Features](features.md#group-the-table).
1. On **Releases**, use the timeline to see when each release ships. See [Releases](releases.md#use-the-timeline).
1. To see only what's approved, choose **Live only** in the **Draft** menu above the table. See [Drafts](drafts.md#what-the-draft-menu-shows).

## Look at People

1. On the **Org chart**, find a person or team. See [Org chart](org-chart.md#find-a-person-or-team).
1. To see the chart on another day, choose the date. See [Org chart](org-chart.md#see-another-date).
1. On **People**, select **Metrics** to count people by a column, such as level or site. See [People](people.md#count-people).

## Follow What Changed

1. On **Change history**, search for a person, a value or who made the change. See [Change history](change-history.md#find-a-change).
1. On **Access history**, see who changed an account, a role or a setting. See [Access history](access-history.md).
1. Under **Administration**, look at the accounts, roles and settings. You can see them, but not change them.

## Keep a View

A view is how you've set up a table, such as its search and filters. You can save one on **People**, **Feature assignments** or **Capacity**'s **People** to come back to. See [Saved views](saved-views.md#save-a-view).

You're done when you've found what you came for.
