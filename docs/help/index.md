# Help

How to use each page of Fathom, and the work each role does most.

## Working in Fathom

- [Setting up Fathom](setting-up-fathom.md): a new Fathom, from an empty database to an organization ready to plan.
- [How work moves between roles](how-work-moves-between-roles.md): a change drafted, approved, and staffed.
- [Working as a Team Member](working-as-a-team-member.md): the plan, projects and people, to read.
- [Working as a Product Manager](working-as-a-product-manager.md): features, projects, proposals and releases.
- [Working as a Feature Lead](working-as-a-feature-lead.md): a feature team's features, estimates and members.
- [Working as a Project Manager](working-as-a-project-manager.md): projects to delivery, and approving drafts.
- [Working as an Engineering Manager](working-as-an-engineering-manager.md): feature teams, allocations and estimates.
- [Working as an Engineering Director](working-as-an-engineering-director.md): approving, reporting lines, hiring and data classes.
- [Working as a Workforce Administrator](working-as-a-workforce-administrator.md): people, the hiring plan, lists and feature teams.
- [Working as an Executive](working-as-an-executive.md): the plan, the organization and what changed, to read.
- [Working as an Administrator](working-as-an-administrator.md): accounts, roles, settings and the access history.

## Plan

- [Overview](overview.md): the page Fathom opens on.
- [Projects](projects.md): projects, proposals, customers and milestones.
- [Features](features.md): every feature planned, grouped by cycle.
- [Releases](releases.md): release streams, their cycles and releases, and the timeline.

## People

- [People](people.md): everyone in the organization and the planned hires, as a table.
- [Org chart](org-chart.md): reporting teams and feature teams as cards, on any date.
- [Feature assignments](feature-assignments.md): who is on which feature team, month by month.
- [Capacity](capacity.md): what each person works on, month by month, and the planning settings capacity and forecasts are worked out with.
- [Lists](lists.md): the lists of choices that columns offer.
- [Drafts page](drafts-page.md): the drafts waiting, and approving or rejecting them.

## Tables

- [Saved views](saved-views.md): saving, sharing and applying a table's views, on People for now.

## Changes

- [Drafts](drafts.md): what the marks on a draft mean, what happens to it, and undoing a change.

## History

- [Change history](change-history.md): every change saved, who made it, and the values before and after.
- [Access history](access-history.md): sign-ins, changes to accounts, roles and settings, and who viewed or exported private details.

## Administration

- [Accounts](accounts.md): who can sign in, and the roles each holds.
- [Roles](roles.md): what each role lets its holders see and do, and changing it.
- [Settings](settings.md): the time zone and the rules for passwords.
- [Data classes](data-classes.md): the class of each detail, column and list, and changing it.
