# Fathom: Issues

**Status:** Working list
**Last updated:** 2026-11-10

Known problems and deferred work that are not yet scheduled in the [Implementation Plan](design/implementation-plan.md). Each item says why it was deferred. When an item is scheduled or resolved, it moves to the document that owns it, or is removed.

Each item starts with a label, such as **Bug · P3** or **Debt · Next**. A **Bug** takes its priority from its probability (Likely, Possible, or Rare) and its severity (Severe, Moderate, or Minor), as the table below sets out. P1 is fixed next, P2 in the next change that touches its area, and P3 and P4 in a cleanup pass. Any other item is **Quality of life**, **Feature**, or **Debt**, with **Now** for the current work, **Next** for soon after, **Later** for when its area is next changed or it is wanted, and **Maybe** for only if a need arises.

| Probability | Severe | Moderate | Minor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Likely | P1 | P2 | P3 |
| Possible | P2 | P3 | P4 |
| Rare | P3 | P4 | P4 |

## Access History

- **Feature · Maybe** **Sign-ins, sign-outs, and failed sign-ins:** Not recorded in the access history. The server's request log already records each sign-in attempt with its time and network address, and active sessions are kept in the database. Deferred by the owner; worth revisiting if HR or IT asks for them.
- **Bug · P3** **One name for Restricted and Confidential views:** The server's `includedRestricted` flags, on the org chart, People and the hiring plan, are also set when Confidential details are shown, and are recorded as `restricted-viewed`, so the names say less than they do. Found in review on 2026-11-07.

## Roles

- **Feature · Next** **Access limited to one's own part of the organization:** Permissions apply to everyone's data, so Engineering Managers see Restricted details for everyone, and a Project Manager cannot be limited to editing their own projects. The owner decided on 2026-11-04 that permissions will take a scope, such as one's own reporting line, and that it must be built; it waits until roles are editable and Teams and Reporting Lines is built, and may use the linked person records proposed in [Roles](product/roles.md#access-to-your-own-record-and-your-reports).
- **Feature · Maybe** **A second person confirming Confidential grants:** A change granting Confidential data, to a role or to an account's role, takes effect at once, without a second person confirming it. It is recorded in the access history and marked there as worth checking, as is every account created. Deferred by the owner on 2026-11-05, since few applications have such a check; built if HR asks for it.
- **Bug · P4** **Unusual permission sets offer what the server refuses:** Some permissions need another that the Roles page does not name, so a role holding one without the other is shown actions the server then refuses. No starting role is affected. Edit Configuration cannot make or change a list without View People, yet the Lists page offers **New list** and **Edit**. Edit Configuration with View People, but not Edit People, is offered **Delete column** for a live column, which the server refuses. Edit People says it changes reporting lines, but changing a manager needs Edit Restricted details too, since reporting lines' dates are Restricted, and the People grid still lets the manager be changed. Feature team permissions need both View People and View Org chart, while the Roles page can name only one permission a permission does nothing without. Found by the permissions tests on 2026-11-05.

- **Quality of life · Next** **Viewing that comes from another row of the Roles grid:** Releases, Feature teams, Features, and Team colours have no View box, since they are seen through View on another row: releases with the plan or configuration, feature teams and team colours with the org chart, and features with the plan. A role granting Approve on Releases, for example, shows Approve alone, which reads as approving what the role cannot see. The owner decided on 2026-11-09 to show a greyed View box on those rows, ticked when the row granting the view is ticked, and otherwise empty, with a tooltip naming that row, and to widen the line under the grid to say a greyed tick can come from another area. Next after the allocations server change.
- **Bug · P3** **Refusals shown in general words:** The web shows every refusal for lack of permission as "Not allowed for this account. Its roles may have changed.", so a refusal the server words for the user, such as that adding people needs seeing positions, or that an Approver who sees a draft's details must decide it, never reaches the screen. Found while letting the built-in lists' visibility change, on 2026-11-05.
- **Bug · P3** **One tier for each recorded change:** A change, a rejection, and a cleared rejection each record one tier, the most sensitive of the fields they cover, while each tier is a permission of its own, so a role that sees Confidential but not Restricted data could see a rejection reason recorded at Confidential for a draft that also changed a Restricted detail. Fathom's starting roles that see Confidential data also see Restricted data, so none is affected today. Found on 2026-11-05.

## Planning

- **Bug · P4** **Cycles from the 29th to the 31st:** A monthly cycle that starts on one of these days starts on the last day of shorter months. Changing one such cycle's length counts the cycles after it from that cycle's own start, so they can stay on the 28th from then on. Deferred by the owner on 2026-11-05 as rare in practice.
- **Feature · Maybe** **Freezes between cycles:** A stream's cycles follow one another with no gap, so a freeze, such as the end of December, can only be a short cycle with a name and number of its own. Deferred by the owner on 2026-11-04 until a stream needs one; a cycle marked as a gap, unnamed and not counted, would fit the cycle patterns.
- **Quality of life · Maybe** **Undoing changes to the plan's records:** The server refuses undoing any change to the plan's records or to allocations, since undoing one can break the rules for cycles and releases, or the links between records, that approving checks. The tables still offer Undo after such a change, which then fails. Deferred until undo is wanted for them.
- **Debt · Next** **No limit on a request's lists of records:** The lists of records a request picks, such as a project's features or a feature's projects, read by `picked` and `pickedIfNamed` in `packages/shared/src/record-fields.ts`, have no largest length, unlike other lists of identifiers in `packages/shared`. Found at the code review on 2026-11-10.
- **Quality of life · Maybe** **Ordering release streams:** Streams keep the order they were added in; moving them, as the Decisions Log allows, is not built yet. Deferred until an organization has enough streams for the order to matter, since the monthly resource grid's picker defaults to the first.
- **Feature · Maybe** **Which delivery a feature is needed by:** The release warning checks each needed feature against its project's earliest customer delivery date still to come, so a phased project warns about features only its later deliveries need. Saying which delivery each feature is needed by was deferred by the owner on 2026-11-03 until the warnings prove noisy.

- **Feature · Next** **Feature assignments page, still to come:** Kept out of its first, read-only form by the owner on 2026-11-08, to keep that change small: saved views, which the server keeps only for People today; added columns, where any column on People can be added, perhaps with a maximum until pages can be copied with different setups; a Today button; vertical lines for one release stream's cycle ends and releases; and a filter for a feature wherever it shows on the grid, in the bar above the table rather than a column.
- **Quality of life · Later** **Resizing many columns:** Each column is resized on its own, and the Views menu lists every resized column on one Widths line, which grows with each. Bulk resizing was raised by the owner on 2026-11-08; whether to shorten that line is not decided.
- **Quality of life · Next** **A table reopens at its last view:** A table's view, its search, filters, sorting, grouping and columns, is kept only in the page address, so opening the page again from the left-hand menu starts it afresh, and only a saved view brings one back. Each table could reopen at the view it last showed, kept like a saved view. Raised by the owner on 2026-11-10 while checking Features grouped by release, for a pull request of its own.

- **Debt · Later** **Tables without every editing member:** A table that changes only some things, such as Feature assignments, which changes cells but never adds or removes records, must still supply the shared table's editing and draft members as stubs that are never called, with a `withDrafts` option to hide its Draft menu. Letting such a table leave them out would reshape the types every table uses, so it is its own change. Found in review on 2026-11-09.
- **Bug · P4** **Dates in the years 0 to 99:** `addMonths` in `packages/shared/src/cycles.ts` reads them as the 1900s. The monthly grid's query refuses them; other callers take dates people enter. Found in review on 2026-11-09.
- **Debt · Next** **Two meanings of a month's number:** `apps/web/src/timeline.ts` has a private `monthNumber` that counts differently from the shared one in `packages/shared/src/days.ts`. Found in review on 2026-11-09.

- **Feature · Next** **Making drafts is not checked:** Draft on Drafts, which says it makes drafts of changes to the plan and allocations, is still marked Not built yet on the Roles page, since changing the plan or allocations needs only Edit on their own areas. Checking it means deciding what a role with Edit but not Draft may do. Found while building allocations on 2026-11-09.
- **Quality of life · Now** **Allocations named only by their feature:** A user's list of unpublished changes names a change to an allocation as "an allocation to the feature …", without who is allocated, since the plan's records are named from the plan alone. To be settled with allocating from the grid, which brings allocation drafts to the screens. Found on 2026-11-09.
- **Debt · Later** **Every plan change loads every allocation:** `readRecordsState` in `apps/server/src/data/records.ts` loads the whole allocations table, and every allocation draft, whenever any of the plan's records is read, drafted, published, approved or rejected, since the rules for removing a feature or project read allocations from the same state. Allocations will outnumber every other kind of record, so plan changes slow as they grow. Loading them only for the features, projects or people a change touches must keep those rules whole. Accepted by the owner on 2026-11-10 as a concern to revisit.
- **Bug · P3** **Allocations of people who have left, for readers without Restricted details:** The Allocations mode of Feature assignments has the rows the Teams mode has, so these readers see only people employed today, and not the past months of those who have left, which [Capacity views](product/decisions.md#planning) counts. To be settled with the capacity total rows, which need them. Found while building the Allocations mode on 2026-11-10.
- **Bug · P2** **Planned hires counted as people in Allocations:** Metrics on Feature assignments, in the Allocations mode, counts planned hires as people, such as "40 people" with a planned hire among them, where People says "150 people and 6 planned hires". Deferred by the owner on 2026-11-10 to the capacity total rows, which count planned hires apart.
- **Debt · Next** **Allocation drafts have no manual test yet:** Nothing on screen makes an allocation draft until allocating from a cell is built, and the seed holds none, so [Feature Assignments](testing/feature-assignments-manual-test.md) has no case for how they show; automated tests cover them until then. ## Account Administration

- **Bug · P4** **Two administrators choosing the same email address at once:** Two accounts given the same email address at the same moment, by creating or changing them, can give a server error instead of the "already exists" message. Accepted by the owner as unlikely with 20 to 30 users.
- **Bug · P4** **Changing a password after deactivation:** Changing one's own password goes straight to Better Auth, which does not check that the account is still active, so a session made just before an account is deactivated could still change its password. The session cannot be used for anything else. Found in review on 2026-11-03.
- **Bug · P4** **Two users taking the same name at once:** The check that a name is free when users change their own takes no lock, so two users renaming themselves to the same name at the same moment could both succeed, and Administrators creating or renaming accounts do not check names at all. Both renames are still recorded and marked as worth checking. Accepted as unlikely with 20 to 30 users, like the email address case above. Found in review on 2026-11-03.

## Lists

The switch stays once column filters come. Asked for by the owner on 2026-11-06, to follow planned hires as rows of People.
- **Debt · Later** **Splitting the lists module:** `apps/server/src/data/lists.ts` holds both reading lists with their drafts and drafting changes to them, and is large enough to split. Deferred by the owner on 2026-11-01 as low risk at Fathom's size.
- **Debt · Later** **Reading drafts' history:** Reading lists or people reads each draft's whole history of changes, to name who changed it, and undoing changes to drafts of lists reads every list again for each record undone. Both are fast at Fathom's size; deferred by the owner on 2026-11-01 until tables grow.
- **Bug · P3** **Undoing a list's creation:** Undoing a change set that created a live list deletes the list without checking that nothing draws on it, so undoing an old change set that created what is now the built-in Positions list, in a database with no people, would remove it. Such change sets predate drafts, so few databases hold one; found by review on 2026-11-04, older than the built-in lists.

- **Bug · P3** **Visibility of feature and proposal statuses:** The Decisions Log says a list's visibility sets who sees its column's values, but everyone who sees the plan is sent the names of feature statuses and proposal statuses, whatever visibility those lists are set to. Neither holds sensitive data today. Either apply the list's tier or treat the plan's lists as General and stop offering another tier. Deferred by the owner on 2026-11-08, to settle both lists together.
